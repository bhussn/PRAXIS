# PRAXIS

PRAXIS is a personalized career-intelligence platform built for college students navigating the transition from education to the workforce. College provides students with the foundation of their field, but rapidly changing technologies, regulations, and industry trends can make it difficult for curricula to keep pace with what employers need. Students are expected to stay informed while balancing classes, work, internships, and other responsibilities, yet thousands of articles are published every day, making it difficult to identify what actually matters, understand its impact on their career, and determine what to do next.

PRAXIS addresses this gap by tailoring current industry news to a student's major and career interests, then transforming each development into an easy-to-understand experience that follows:

**News → TLDR → Relevance → Career Impact → Action**

Instead of simply reporting what happened, PRAXIS explains why a development matters to the student's field, how it could affect their career, and what steps they can take to stay prepared. For example, a computer science student might encounter a story about a new AI coding agent and learn not only what was released, but how AI-assisted development is changing software engineering and what skills they can develop in response.

Unlike traditional news aggregators, PRAXIS connects industry information directly to the individual student and provides a clear direction forward. Students don't just learn what is happening in their industry; they understand **why it matters to them and what they can do about it**.

## Built With

- **Figma** — UI/UX design and prototyping
- **React** — Frontend application
- **Tailwind CSS** — Responsive styling and interface design
- **Supabase** — Authentication, database, and user data
- **Cloudflare** — Application hosting and infrastructure
- **Claude** — Industry analysis and personalized career guidance

## How It Works

1. **Personalize** — Students select their major, career interests, and concerns.
2. **Collect** — PRAXIS gathers current industry news from sources organized by career category.
3. **Process** — Articles are processed and ranked based on relevance.
4. **Explain** — PRAXIS generates concise summaries and career-focused analysis.
5. **Take Action** — Students receive actionable guidance based on how developments may affect their career.

Ultimately, PRAXIS is designed to help students understand where their industries are heading, prepare for those changes, and **bridge the gap between what they learn today and the workforce they will enter tomorrow.**
